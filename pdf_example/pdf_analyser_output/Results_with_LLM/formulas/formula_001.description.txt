Y' computation at spatial locations and channels

The formula represents a mathematical operation that computes the value of Y' at spatial location (h, w) and channel c. It involves two nested summations over indices i and j from 0 to D_h -1 and D_w -1 respectively. The expression inside the summation is X[h + i, w + j, c] multiplied by F_depthwise[i, j, c]. This operation is performed for each spatial location (h, w) and channel c.
